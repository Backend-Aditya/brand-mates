# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Australian founders (Seed to Series C) and CMOs at Blackbird, Square Peg, and AirTree-backed companies, plus pre-ASX-IPO and ASX-listed businesses considering a rebrand. They arrive mid-crisis or mid-growth: either the brand has outgrown the founding story, or they are about to raise and need the brand to do work it currently cannot do. They have been burned by offshore agencies and pitch theatre before. They want a partner, not a vendor. They read the work before they read the words.

## Product Purpose

BrandMates is a senior Australian brand studio based in Sydney and Melbourne, offering brand strategy, identity, web, social media, paid advertising, and content creation. The product is the outcome for the client brand: sharper positioning, higher perceived value, and a visual system their team can run without the studio holding their hand. Success means a client who refers two more clients, not one who asks for revisions.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, Warm, Distinctive.

Rigour comes from specificity: Blackbird-backed, AEST hours, APRA-licensed, Surry Hills. Warmth comes from honesty: we will point you to another studio if we are not the right fit. Distinctiveness comes from contrast: a senior-only boutique in a market full of large agencies and solo freelancers.

Voice: Direct, no-nonsense, Australian. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No buzzwords. The studio sounds like a person you would trust with your category, not a company that hired a copywriter.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS agency sites: navy or teal palette, hero stats grid, cookie-cutter case study thumbnails, "We help brands grow" headlines. This is the entire market default.
- Dark-mode portfolio showreels: all-black canvas, oversized kinetic type, nothing but vibes and no legible information about what the studio actually does or who they serve.
- Warm/beige editorial studios (Pentagram, Collins): the cream-and-black editorial aesthetic signals global prestige but cold distance. BrandMates is warm and local, not reverent and remote.

## Design Principles

1. **Earned authority, not asserted authority.** Never claim "world-class" or "leading." Show the specific work, the specific client, the specific market, and let those speak. Bragging in adjectives is the thing we are replacing.
2. **Warmth through specificity.** Emotion comes from concrete detail, not from adjectives. "Surry Hills seed rounds to ASX-listed rebrands" is warm. "Passionate about brands" is not.
3. **Senior craft as proof of product.** The site is the sample work. Every motion choice, typographic decision, and layout call is a portfolio piece. Lazy defaults undercut the pitch.
4. **Accountability at every touchpoint.** Real phone numbers, real addresses, real names and cities. The studio's credibility depends on being findable and reachable, not behind a contact form.
5. **Local by design, not by slogan.** AEST hours, ABN, AUD pricing, EOFY and AFL/NRL awareness built in. Australia-first is operational, not a tagline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AAA where achievable; AA as the non-negotiable floor. Body text must clear 7:1 against background; large text 4.5:1. Reduced-motion alternative required for every animation. Keyboard navigation fully operational across all interactive elements. Focus indicators visible and high-contrast. All form fields labelled, all icon buttons named with aria-label.
